Roof replacement in Knoxville.

A full tear-off and new roof, with the price in writing before anyone climbs a ladder.

What it is

A replacement strips the old shingles down to the deck, repairs any bad decking, and installs new underlayment, flashing, ventilation and shingles. It is the right call when repairs would only be patching a roof that is finished.

When you need one

  • Shingles are curling, cracking, or losing granules across large areas.
  • You have leaks in more than one spot, or a repair that keeps coming back.
  • A storm damaged enough of the roof that patching is not a real fix. See storm damage and insurance.
  • The roof is at the end of its life and you would rather choose the timing than have a leak choose it.

How we do it

  1. We inspect the roof, the attic side when we can get to it, and the gutters.
  2. We photograph what we find and show you every picture.
  3. You get a written estimate that says what is included.
  4. If you say yes, we put a crew on the schedule and give you the date.
  5. When the work is done, we walk it with you and send final photos.

In the City of Knoxville a re-roof needs a building permit, and the city inspects after tear-off and before the new roof goes on. Permit processing is typically one to three business days. We plan for that in the schedule.

What it costs in Knoxville

For an asphalt-shingle home, a replacement typically runs $10,000 to $18,000. That is a derived range, not a quote: roughly 25 to 30 squares of roof (a square is 100 square feet) at local per-square prices. Your number depends on:

  • The size and pitch of the roof. Steeper roofs take more labor.
  • How many layers are on it now. Each extra layer adds tear-off work.
  • Decking that has rotted and has to be replaced.
  • Ventilation and flashing work the roof needs.
  • The shingle grade you choose.

Our roof cost guide goes through each of these. Winter is not a stop sign here. Shingle makers say that below about 40 degrees the factory seal strips may not bond on their own, so the shingles get hand-sealed with roof cement. It is slower, and we plan for it.

Common questions

How long does a replacement take?

Most single-family roofs are a matter of days once the crew is on site, with weather the main variable. We give you the start date when you approve the estimate, and we tell you if rain moves it.

Do I have to be home?

No. We need access to the driveway and the yard. We will walk the finished roof with you afterward, and you can do that over video or in person.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

We can tell you the typical range. A real number needs an inspection, because pitch, layers and decking change the price more than the square footage does.
