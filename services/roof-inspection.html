Home/Roof inspection

Free roof inspection in Knoxville.

About an hour on site. Photos of everything we find, and a written estimate if there is work to do.

What it is

We go over the whole roof: shingles, flashing at chimneys, vents and valleys, the roof edge, gutters, and from the attic side if we can reach it. We are looking for storm damage, wear, and the things that lead to leaks.

When you need one

  • After hail or a bad wind storm.
  • Before you buy or sell a house.
  • You have a stain on a ceiling and do not know where it comes from.
  • The roof is old and you want to know how much time it has.

How we do it

  1. We inspect the roof, the attic side when we can get to it, and the gutters.
  2. We photograph what we find and show you every picture.
  3. You get a written estimate that says what is included.
  4. If you say yes, we put a crew on the schedule and give you the date.
  5. When the work is done, we walk it with you and send final photos.

You keep the photos and the estimate whether or not you hire us. If the roof is fine, we tell you so.

What it costs

The inspection is free. Any repair or replacement that follows is priced in writing. For context, a replacement on an asphalt-shingle home typically runs $10,000 to $18,000, and a phone quote cannot tell you where yours falls. That is why we look first. See the cost guide.

If you think storm damage is involved, do the inspection before you decide anything about a claim. Here is how the claim process works.

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

It helps for the first few minutes and the walkthrough, but we do not need to be in your house. We need access to the yard and, if we are checking the attic, an attic hatch.

Do you get on the roof?

Yes, when it is safe. In wet or icy conditions or on very steep roofs we may work from a ladder and from the ground, and we will tell you what we could not reach.

Is there any obligation?

No. You do not owe us anything for the inspection and you do not have to hire us.
