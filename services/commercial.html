Where we are

Crestone Roofing does commercial and multifamily roofing in Colorado. The Knoxville operation is new, and we are putting residential roofing first. Commercial work in Knoxville begins after the residential side is running.

If you have a building now

If you manage an apartment community, a small commercial building, or a property portfolio in the Knoxville area, send us an inquiry. Tell us the address, the roof type if you know it, and what is going on: leaks, storm damage, a replacement you are planning. We will tell you plainly whether we can take it on yet, and when.

The same process applies: inspect, photograph, written estimate, scheduled crew, final walkthrough with photos.

We would rather say "not yet" than take a job we are not ready for. Residential service is on the roof replacement and roof repair pages.

Tennessee contractor licensing is in progress. We will post the license number on the About page when the state issues it. Until then, what work we can take on is limited, and we will tell you exactly what that means for your job.
