Why homeowners pick metal

Metal sheds the heavy rain and storms East Tennessee is known for and can last decades with very little upkeep.

  • Standing seam with hidden fasteners for a clean look
  • Exposed-fastener panels for barns, shops and budget-friendly cabins
  • A wide range of colors
  • Proper underlayment, trim and snow guards where they help

Shingle or metal?

We price both at your inspection so you can compare cost and lifespan side by side.

Common questions

Is metal louder in the rain?

Over solid decking and underlayment, most homeowners notice very little difference.

Does metal cost more?

Usually more up front than shingles, and it often lasts much longer. We'll price both.
