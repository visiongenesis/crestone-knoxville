Roof repair in Knoxville.

If a repair will do, we say so. If it will not, we show you why.

What it is

A repair fixes a specific problem: a leak at a vent pipe or chimney, a patch of missing or lifted shingles, a failed section of flashing, a soft spot in the deck. It leaves the rest of the roof alone.

When you need one

  • You see a water stain on a ceiling, or you hear dripping in a storm.
  • Shingles blew off or lifted after wind.
  • Flashing around a chimney, skylight, or wall has pulled loose.
  • A branch hit the roof.

Leaks rarely show up where the water gets in. We trace it before we quote it, because a patch in the wrong place is money spent.

How we do it

  1. We inspect the roof, the attic side when we can get to it, and the gutters.
  2. We photograph what we find and show you every picture.
  3. You get a written estimate that says what is included.
  4. If you say yes, we put a crew on the schedule and give you the date.
  5. When the work is done, we walk it with you and send final photos.

Repair or replace?

If the damage is local and the rest of the roof has years left, a repair is the honest answer. If the shingles are brittle across the whole roof, or the same repair keeps coming back, we show you the photos and explain why replacing is cheaper over time. Either way the choice is yours.

One caution on insurance: Tennessee matching rules apply to replacements, not repairs. If storm damage is behind the leak, read how a roof claim works before you pay for a repair, and photograph everything first.

What it costs

Repairs are priced after the inspection, in writing, because a flashing fix and a deck repair are very different jobs. We do not quote a repair price over the phone.

Common questions

Can you stop an active leak right away?

If water is coming in, call. A temporary tarp or sealing can limit damage until the real repair. Keep any photos and receipts, because they matter if insurance is involved.

Will a repair match my existing shingles?

Often close, not always exact. Older shingles weather, and some product lines are discontinued. We tell you before the work if the match will be visible.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. You get the photos and the written estimate whether or not you hire us.
