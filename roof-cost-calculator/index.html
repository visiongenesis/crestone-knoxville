How we figure it

We start with the footprint of the house, the ground the roof sits over. A pitch factor turns that into roof surface: 1.06 for a low roof, 1.16 for a typical one and 1.3 for a steep one. A shape factor adds the valleys, dormers and hips: 1.00 for a simple roof, 1.08 for an average one and 1.18 for a cut-up one. Then we add 10 percent for waste, the pieces cut off at edges and valleys. Roofers count area in squares, and one square is 100 square feet.

Each square is figured at $600 to $1,000 for architectural asphalt shingles and $1,200 to $1,800 for standing seam metal. A second old layer to tear off adds $75 to $125 a square. Both ends of the range are rounded to the nearest $500.

Left out: rotted decking (priced per sheet once the old roof is off), gutters and permit fees. These are planning assumptions for the Knoxville area, not Crestone's prices. Cost questions

How much does a roof replacement cost in Knoxville?

Most asphalt shingle replacements on Knoxville-area homes of about 2,000 square feet land around $15,000 to $30,000. Size and pitch, a second layer to tear off, rotted decking and the shingle line you pick all move the number. Your free inspection gets you a written price, line by line, so you can see where the money goes. For a planning range on your own house, try the roof cost calculator.

Why do roofing quotes vary so much?

Because they often aren't pricing the same roof. One quote includes full tear-off, ice-and-water membrane in the valleys, and fresh pipe boots and flashing. Another reuses the flashing, skips the membrane and bills rotted decking later. Ask each roofer for a line-by-line price and compare line to line.

Will the price change once you start?

Only for one thing nobody can see until the old roof is off: rotted decking. Your written price lists a cost per sheet up front, and only boards that are actually soft get replaced. Everything else on the price is fixed. If anything else came up, you'd decide before any work on it went ahead.
