Farragut, TN

Roofing in Farragut, from Concord Hills to Kingston Pike

Farragut roofs deal with wind off Fort Loudoun Lake, summer storms rolling in from the west, and in a lot of neighborhoods, an HOA that wants a say in your shingle color. Here's how we handle all three.

Farragut at a glance

  • Incorporated in 1980, after Knoxville tried to annex land along Kingston Pike. About 23,500 people at the 2020 census.
  • Mostly in Knox County, with the western edge in Loudon County.
  • Between Blackoak Ridge to the north and Fort Loudoun Lake to the south.
  • Neighborhoods include Concord Hills, Thornton Heights, Fox Den and Village Green. Concord, the village founded in 1854 just southeast of town, has older homes and older roofs.

Source: Wikipedia, Farragut, unless noted.

What Farragut roofs are up against

A roof put on in the late 1990s or early 2000s is now 20 to 25 years old, and plenty of those are on their first replacement. Summer thunderstorms are the main threat. Knox County saw hail up to 1.5 inches on September 4, 2025 (WBIR) and an EF-2 tornado on August 7, 2023 (National Weather Service, Morristown). Homes on the lake side take more wind across open water. Wind lifts the tabs on older shingles and breaks their seal, and the next storm peels them back.

The dark streaks on north-facing slopes are algae. They're mostly cosmetic, but they're a reason to pick an algae-resistant shingle when you replace.

Your HOA comes first

Many Farragut subdivisions have covenants on roof color and material. Find your HOA's review form. We'll fill in the shingle maker, line and color, with a sample photo, and order only after approval comes back in writing.

Permits in Farragut

Farragut runs its own codes office, separate from the City of Knoxville. The town says a plain re-roof usually doesn't need a building permit unless the job includes structural work. We check for your address, and if a permit is needed, we pull it.

Common questions

Will my HOA approve a different shingle color?

It depends on your covenants. Some Farragut HOAs list approved colors, some only ask that the color fit the street, and a few want the same color you have now. Bring the manufacturer, the shingle line and the exact color name to the review form, plus a photo of a sample. We supply all three, and we don't order material until the approval is in writing.

Do I need a permit in Farragut?

Usually not. The Town of Farragut says a plain re-roof doesn't typically need a building permit, unless the job needs structural work. Farragut runs its own codes office, separate from the City of Knoxville. If your roof does need a permit, we pull it, put the fee as a line on your price, and show you the permit card on the job.

After a storm, should I call my insurance company or a roofer first?

Get a free inspection first. It tells you whether there's damage worth a claim. If there is, you file it with your insurer, since it's your claim, and we can document the damage, write a scope and meet the adjuster at the house. If there isn't, you've spent an hour and nothing else.

Will my HOA have a say in my roof?

In a lot of Knoxville-area subdivisions, yes. Many covenants set rules on shingle color and sometimes material, and the HOA wants its review form signed off before work starts. We'll fill in the manufacturer, line and color for the form, and material gets ordered only after the HOA says yes in writing.
