Roofing in Oak Ridge.

Wartime housing from the 1940s on one side of town, newer subdivisions on the other, and very different roofs on each.

The houses

Oak Ridge was built fast in the 1940s, and much of that wartime housing is still standing: small single-story homes, many with simple, low-slope roofs. They were designed to go up quickly, not to carry a roof for eighty years. Owners often find that the decking and the flashing, not just the shingles, need attention. Some of these houses are small enough that a re-roof is a one-day job once the old roof is off, and some have additions that changed the roofline over the years.

The newer west end has later subdivisions with conventional pitches and larger roofs. A newer house and a wartime house are two different jobs, and we price them as such. Photos of the existing roof help us give you a first idea of cost before we visit, but only the inspection gives you a real number.

Weather and trees

Oak Ridge is heavily wooded, with long ridges and many lots under tall trees. Falling limbs and leaf buildup in valleys and gutters cause more trouble than anything else. Wind comes with summer thunderstorms. Hail is possible but occasional.

What we do

We inspect, photograph and write the estimate. On a low-slope or older roof we check the decking closely and show you what we find. A typical asphalt re-roof in this area runs $10,000 to $18,000. A small wartime house can come in below that, and a large newer house above it. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Permits

Oak Ridge has its own city codes office, and the city sits across more than one county line. We find out which office covers your address and handle the re-roof permit.

Questions people ask here

My house is a 1940s original. Is the roof still the original?

Almost never. Most have been replaced several times. We look at how many layers are on it and what is underneath.

Low-slope roofs worry me. Do they need special materials?

Very low pitches can limit which shingles a manufacturer allows. We check the slope before we recommend anything.

Do you cover the west end too?

Yes. All of Oak Ridge is inside our service area.
