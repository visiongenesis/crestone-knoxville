Twenty years of growth, twenty-year roofs

Lenoir City grew by about a quarter in the 2000s. Many of the roofs put on in that growth are now at or near 20 years old: the age when granules wash into the gutters, seals let go in the wind, and the first leaks show up. A drone inspection tells you whether yours needs a repair or is ready to be replaced.

Wind off the lake

Homes along the river and the lake take wind straight off open water. Wind lifts the edges and ridges first. If you're on the water, ask us to look at those closely, and pick a shingle with a strong wind rating at replacement.

HOAs and subdivisions

Many of the subdivisions here have covenants on roof color and material. We give you the manufacturer, line and color name for the review form, and order material after approval is in writing.

Common questions

Is the drone inspection really free?

Yes. There's no charge and nothing to sign. We fly your roof, take close-up photos of every slope, and go through them with you. The photos are yours whether you hire us or not. If your roof is in good shape, we'll tell you that and leave.

After a storm, should I call my insurance company or a roofer first?

We'd get a free inspection first. It tells you whether there's damage worth a claim before anything goes on your record. If there is, you file the claim with your insurer (it's your claim) and we'll document the damage, write the scope, and meet the adjuster on the roof with you. If there isn't, you've lost nothing but an hour.

Which areas do you cover?

Knoxville and the towns around it: Farragut, Hardin Valley, Karns, Powell, Oak Ridge, Maryville, Lenoir City, Sevierville and Seymour. That covers parts of Knox, Blount, Loudon, Anderson and Sevier counties. If you're just outside that list, text us your address and we'll tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

Will my HOA have a say in my roof?

In a lot of Knoxville-area subdivisions, yes. Many covenants set rules on shingle color and sometimes material, and the HOA wants an approval form before work starts. We give you the manufacturer, line and color name, and a sample photo, for the form. We order material after you have approval in writing, so nobody has to tear off a roof the HOA rejects.
