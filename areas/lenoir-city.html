Roofing in Lenoir City.

In-town homes, newer subdivisions and lake houses, with more open exposure to wind than the suburbs closer to Knoxville.

The houses

Lenoir City is in Loudon County, southwest of Knoxville along the Tennessee River and Fort Loudoun Lake. The town has older houses in and around the center, newer subdivisions that have grown up around it, and lake homes with big roof planes and covered porches. The mix means we see everything from small one-story houses to large custom builds.

Lake homes tend to have more complex rooflines and taller walls, so the work takes more setup. Many are second homes, so we can coordinate around your schedule if you are not there during the week, and send the photos by email instead.

Weather

Open water gives wind a longer run at the shoreline, so waterfront and ridge-top homes feel storms more than houses tucked into a subdivision. Wind lifts shingles at edges and ridges. Falling limbs matter, too, especially where lots back up to wooded shoreline. Hail is occasional, not the standard problem. After a storm, check the ceilings under the longest slopes and the shingles along the shoreline side of the house.

What we do and what it costs

We inspect the roof, photograph what we find and give you a written estimate. The typical asphalt re-roof price range is $10,000 to $18,000. Complicated lake-house roofs, steep pitches and metal cost more. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Permits

Loudon County and the City of Lenoir City each have a codes office. We find out which one covers your address and handle the permit.

Questions people ask here

How far out do you travel?

Lenoir City is inside our service area. A longer drive from Knoxville can affect when we can schedule you, but not what the estimate says about the roof. We give you a realistic window when you call.

Is a lake house roof different from any other roof?

The materials are the same. The difference is usually size, pitch and access. Those drive the labor, and we price them separately.

Can you repair a small section after a storm?

Often, yes. If the damage is limited to a few shingles or one flashing, we say so and price only that.
