Roofing in Sevierville.

Mountain cabins, short-term rentals and year-round homes, where steep roofs and hard-to-reach lots change the job.

Cabins, rentals and homes

Sevierville sits at the edge of the Smokies, next to Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg. Alongside regular subdivisions, the county has a large stock of cabins, many of them short-term rentals. Sevier County authorized 1,441 new housing units in 2024, so a lot of this housing is fairly new.

Cabin roofs are often steep, and many are metal or heavy architectural shingle. Steep pitch means roof jacks, harnesses and slower work, so the price per square runs higher than on a flat suburban roof. Metal roofs and steep cabins cost more than the $10,000 to $18,000 typical for an asphalt re-roof on an ordinary house.

Who decides

On a rental cabin, the person who signs may not be the owner you picture. It can be an owner who lives out of state, a rental management company, or a homeowners association with rules on materials and color. We send the same photos and written estimate to whoever needs to approve it, and we plan around guest bookings so nobody has a crew on the roof during a stay.

Weather and access

Mountain and ridge-top lots catch strong wind, and limbs come down in storms. Hail is occasional. Steep gravel drives, tight turns and slopes can also slow the delivery of materials and the removal of old shingles, and we plan for that when we quote. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Permits

Sevier County has its own codes office, separate from Knox County, and the City of Sevierville has its own rules inside city limits. We find out which applies to your address and handle the permit.

Questions people ask here

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We deal with the manager or the owner, whichever you tell us to, and we put dates in writing so bookings are not disturbed.

Metal or shingle for a cabin?

Metal lasts longer and sheds leaves and debris well, and it costs more up front. Architectural shingle is cheaper and easier to repair. We price both and let you choose.

Is a steep roof more dangerous to work on?

It takes more safety gear and more time, which is why it costs more. We don't skip the gear to lower a price.
