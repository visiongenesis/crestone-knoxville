Roofing in Karns.

Established subdivisions and newer streets in northwest Knox County, from the 1970s to the present.

The houses

Karns is in northwest Knox County, west of Powell, and is mostly unincorporated. Homes range from 1970s and 1980s ranches and split-levels to 1990s and 2000s subdivisions, with newer streets built since. A lot of these houses are on their first replacement roof, and some of the oldest are on a second or third. The oldest ones are where we most often find worn flashing around chimneys and plumbing vents.

Most are straightforward: gable roofs, a few valleys, a chimney or two. Straightforward roofs are quicker to inspect and easier to price accurately.

Weather

Wind and falling limbs are the usual damage in Karns. Many lots are wooded and hillside homes are more exposed. After a storm, shingles missing along the ridge or edges, or a limb across the roof, are what we are called about. Hail is an occasional event. If you see granules in the gutters or a stain on a ceiling after a storm, that is worth a look sooner than later.

What we do

We inspect the roof and photograph everything we find, including the attic if you give us access. The written estimate separates repair from replacement, so you can see what each costs. A typical asphalt re-roof in the Knoxville area runs $10,000 to $18,000. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Permits

A re-roof in Knox County needs a permit, and we handle it, from application through inspection.

Questions people ask here

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. There is no charge for the inspection or the written estimate.

What if a tree limb is still on the roof?

Call us. We can look at the damage, and if it's an emergency we'll tell you what to do before the next rain. Cutting up and hauling the limb itself is a tree service's job, not ours.

How long does a re-roof take?

Most single-family roofs are a matter of days once materials are on site, and weather can move that. We give you a start date in writing.
