Roofing in Hardin Valley.

A part of west Knox County built mostly since 2005, now getting to the age where first roofs wear out.

The houses

Hardin Valley, along Hardin Valley Road and the Pellissippi Parkway, grew quickly from the mid-2000s on. The homes are mostly newer two-story houses in planned subdivisions, with architectural shingles, several gables and tight lot lines. Many have never had a roof replaced.

That makes this area different from the older parts of Knoxville. Here the question is less about rot and layers, and more about the first roof reaching the end of its life, often at around 15 to 20 years, depending on the shingle and the sun exposure.

What we look for

On a house from 2005 to 2012 we check for cracking, granule loss in the gutters, lifted tabs, failed flashing at walls and chimneys, and plumbing vent boots, which tend to go before the shingles do. A newer roof with one problem spot gets a repair price, not a pitch for a new roof. We send photos of each spot so you can see what we saw, and we put the repair and replacement numbers side by side in one written estimate.

Weather and permits

Wind is the main risk in Hardin Valley, along with the occasional falling limb. Roofs with several gables and tall walls catch more of it, and new shingles that were never sealed properly can lift in a storm. Hail is possible but not routine. Hardin Valley is in Knox County, and a re-roof needs a county permit. We handle it. Many subdivisions have an HOA with color and style rules, so check yours.

What it costs

A typical asphalt re-roof is $10,000 to $18,000. Larger two-story houses with many valleys cost more. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Questions people ask here

My house is under 20 years old. Why call about a roof?

Because the first small failures, like a vent boot or a bit of flashing, are cheap to fix now and expensive after a leak. The inspection is free.

Will a newer roof need a permit too?

Yes. A replacement in Knox County needs a permit no matter how new the house is. We pull it.

Do you work with HOAs?

Yes. If your HOA has rules, send them and we build the estimate around them.
