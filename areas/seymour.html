Roofing in Seymour.

Rural lots and farmhouse-to-ranch housing southeast of Knoxville, with longer drive times and open wind exposure.

The houses

Seymour is southeast of Knoxville along Chapman Highway, on the Sevier County side of the line. Lots are larger and more rural than in the suburbs, with a mix of older farmhouses, ranches from the 1960s through the 1990s, and newer homes on acreage. Many have outbuildings or detached garages that also have roofs.

Roofs here are often long, plain gables with fewer valleys. Pitch and layout vary more than in a subdivision, so each one gets its own estimate. On a long plain roof, a few bad sections often explain a leak, and we point to them in the photos.

Weather

Open land and ridgelines give wind more room, and limbs from field-edge trees come down in storms. Missing or lifted shingles along ridges and edges are the usual sign, and so is a ceiling stain in the room under a long slope. Hail shows up now and then but is not routine.

Drive times and scheduling

Seymour is farther out, and we say so. A longer drive affects how we schedule inspections and crews, not the quality of the estimate. Tell us your address and we will give you an honest window for the inspection.

What it costs and permits

A typical asphalt re-roof runs $10,000 to $18,000. Metal and steeper roofs cost more, and a second roof on an outbuilding adds to the total. Sevier County has its own codes office, separate from Knox County. We find out what your address needs and handle the permit. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Questions people ask here

Do you charge extra for the drive?

We don't add a mystery fee. Any trip charge or distance factor is written in the estimate.

Can you roof a barn or shop too?

Outbuildings are often roofed with metal or older shingle. Tell us what you have and we will say whether we can quote it.

Is Seymour in Knox County or Sevier County?

Seymour is on the Sevier County side. That matters because the county's codes office, not Knox County's, covers the permit.
