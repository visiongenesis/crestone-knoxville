Maryville sits in the foothills with Chilhowee Mountain to the south. Storms come over the ridges fast, and the homes range from older houses near the college to subdivisions on the edge of town.

Two kinds of Maryville roof

The older homes near the college and downtown often have steeper roofs, older decking and more than one layer. Newer subdivision homes raise a different question: how old the shingles are and what grade they are. Builder-grade shingles past their middle years are where storms start to find weak spots. We price both the same way: tear-off, decking checked board by board, every line written down.

Foothills weather

Being close to the mountains means storms build quickly and roll in over the ridges. Wind finds the edges and ridges first. A drone inspection after a storm shows you the slopes you can't see from the yard, before a small lift turns into a leak.

Permits in Blount County

Blount County publishes its building permits month by month (blounttn.gov/1245/Monthly-Permit-Reports).

Common questions

Is the drone inspection really free?

Yes. There's no charge and nothing to sign. We fly your roof, take close-up photos of every slope, and go through them with you. The photos are yours whether you hire us or not. If your roof is in good shape, we'll tell you that and leave.

After a storm, should I call my insurance company or a roofer first?

We'd get a free inspection first. It tells you whether there's damage worth a claim before anything goes on your record. If there is, you file the claim with your insurer (it's your claim) and we'll document the damage, write the scope, and meet the adjuster on the roof with you. If there isn't, you've lost nothing but an hour.

Which areas do you cover?

Knoxville and the towns around it: Farragut, Hardin Valley, Karns, Powell, Oak Ridge, Maryville, Lenoir City, Sevierville and Seymour. That covers parts of Knox, Blount, Loudon, Anderson and Sevier counties. If you're just outside that list, text us your address and we'll tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

Will my insurance pay for a replacement roof?

It depends on your policy and on what caused the damage. Insurance generally covers sudden damage, such as hail or wind. It usually leaves out wear and age. Your insurer makes that call. What we can do is give your adjuster clear, dated photos of every damaged area and a written scope, so the decision is made on good evidence.
