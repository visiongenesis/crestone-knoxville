Roofing in Farragut.

Large, multi-gable homes in 1980s to 2000s subdivisions, now reaching the age where roofs get a hard look.

The houses

Most of Farragut was built from the 1980s through the 2000s, in subdivisions off Kingston Pike, Concord Road and Campbell Station Road. The homes tend to be large, often two stories, with several gables, dormers, valleys and a steep or moderately steep pitch. More roof area and more valleys means more flashing, more tear-off and more time on the ladder.

That matters for price. A big Farragut house can come in above the typical $10,000 to $18,000 asphalt range, and we say so before you ask.

Age is the story here

Houses from the late 1980s and 1990s are often on their second roof already. Houses from the 2000s are now at the age where the original shingles start to crack, lose granules and let water in around flashing and vents. We look at the condition of the shingles, not just the date on the house.

Weather and trees

Farragut is well wooded, and falling limbs in a storm are the usual source of damage. Wind lifts shingles along ridges and gable edges first. Hail is an occasional event, not something we plan around.

Permits and HOAs

Many Farragut subdivisions have a homeowners association, and some have rules on shingle color or style. Check yours before you choose. The re-roof permit goes through the Town of Farragut or Knox County codes, depending on the address. We find out which and handle it. Our Tennessee licensing is still in progress; the About page says where it stands.

Questions people ask here

My house was built in the 2000s. Is it time for a new roof?

Possibly, but age alone does not decide it. We check for cracked and worn shingles, failed flashing and soft decking, then tell you whether you have a year, five years or more.

Can you match the look of my current shingle?

Usually we can come close. If your HOA requires a specific color or style, send us the rules and we price to them.

Do you work on large two-story homes?

Yes. Tall, complicated roofs need more safety gear and more time, and we put that in the written estimate.
