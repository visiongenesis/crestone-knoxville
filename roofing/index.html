Roofing in Knoxville and East Tennessee

Seven kinds of roofing work, from one cracked pipe boot to a full tear-off on a church hall. Pick the one that sounds like your roof.

Book a free inspectionCall (865) 830-8126Text a photo

Not sure which one you need? Text a photo of what you're seeing to (865) 830-8126, or book a free inspection and find out what the roof needs.

  • Emergency and damage repair

    Water through the ceiling at 2 a.m., a limb through the shingles, a slope stripped by wind. The 24-hour line gets the roof covered and dry first. Then comes the repair, and help with the claim if a storm did it.

  • Roof replacement

    Most asphalt shingle replacements here run $15,000 to $30,000 and take one to three days once materials arrive. The price covers soft decking by the sheet, ice and water shield, flashing, the ridge vent and a magnet sweep of the yard.

  • Metal roofing

    Standing seam hides its fasteners under raised seams and costs more. Exposed-fastener panels go on fast, cost less and suit barns and shops. On a steep Sevier County rental, metal earns its price. Ask for metal and shingles priced side by side.

  • Chimney repair

    Most chimney leaks start at the metal where the roof meets the brick. Step flashing, counterflashing, a cricket behind a wide chimney and a sealed crown keep the water out. Crumbling brick is a mason's job, and we'll tell you when that's what you have.

  • Skylight repair

    A leaking skylight usually has good glass and failed flashing around the frame. Reflashing fixes most of them. When the glass fogs between the panes or the frame rots, the answer is a replacement skylight in the same size, best done during a reroof.

  • Commercial roofing

    Flat and low-slope roofs on stores, offices, warehouses and apartment buildings, in TPO, EPDM, coatings or metal. Dry or wet insulation underneath decides between a coating and a tear-off. Property managers get written prices per building, ready for a budget or a board.

  • Church roofing

    A steep sanctuary, a flat fellowship hall and the valleys between them, each needing a different material. Roof work stops for Sunday. The written price breaks out each roof section, so a building committee can fix the worst one this year and budget the rest.
