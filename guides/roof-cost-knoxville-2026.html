Home/Roof cost in Knoxville, 2026

What a roof costs in Knoxville in 2026.

For an asphalt-shingle home, $10,000 to $18,000 is typical. Here is what moves yours up or down.

The range

A typical asphalt-shingle replacement in Knoxville runs $10,000 to $18,000. This is not an average someone reported. We worked it out: a 2,000 square foot home usually has about 25 to 30 squares of roof (a square is 100 square feet of roof surface, and steeper roofs have more surface than the footprint), and published Knoxville price guides run roughly $400 per square for 3-tab shingles on a single-layer tear-off up to about $580 per square for architectural shingles. Those guides disagree with each other, which is one more reason to treat any range as a starting point.

How roofers price: per square

Most roofers price by the square. Divide a roof into 100 square foot units, multiply by a rate that covers tear-off, materials and labor, then add the pieces that vary: flashing, vents, decking repairs, disposal. When you compare estimates, make sure each one lists the number of squares and what the rate includes.

What moves the price

  • Pitch. A steep roof is slower and riskier to work on, so labor goes up.
  • Layers. A roof with two layers of shingles takes more tear-off and more disposal than one.
  • Decking. Rotted or damaged plywood has to be replaced. You can only know after tear-off, so a good estimate says how decking repairs are priced.
  • Ventilation. Ridge vents, intake vents, and attic airflow affect how the roof ages, and add or fix parts of the job.
  • Material grade. 3-tab, architectural, and premium shingles carry different prices, and so do underlayment options.
  • Metal versus shingle. Metal costs more than shingle on the same house. We have no Knoxville metal price to quote, and we will not guess. See metal roofing.
  • Complexity. Valleys, dormers, chimneys, skylights, and cut-up rooflines add time.
  • Cold weather. Below about 40 degrees, shingle makers call for hand-sealing with roof cement, which is a slower step.

Insurance jobs and retail jobs

A retail job is you paying for a roof you chose. An insurance job is a storm-damage claim where your insurer pays for covered work. The roof costs what the work costs either way. On an insurance job you pay your deductible, and on a replacement-cost policy part of the payment may be held back as depreciation until the work is done. If a contractor offers to waive your deductible, walk away. Our claim guide goes through it.

Financing

Financing for roofing exists in general, through banks, credit unions, and roofing lenders. We have not set up a lender for Knoxville yet and will not name one until we have.

Why one hour on the roof beats a phone quote

Pitch, layers, decking, and ventilation are what change the price, and none of them show up on a map or in a square footage. In about an hour we inspect, photograph what we find, measure, and write a number you can hold us to. Photos also mean you can compare our estimate against someone else's line by line. Common questions

What does a new roof cost in Knoxville?

For an asphalt-shingle home, $10,000 to $18,000 is a typical range. That is derived from published local per-square prices and typical roof sizes, not a promise. Your inspection and written estimate set the real number.

Why not just give me a quote over the phone?

Because pitch, layers, decking, and ventilation change the price, and none of them show up on a satellite photo. We would rather take an hour and give you a real number.

Is financing available?

Roofers and banks offer financing in general. We have not set up a lender for Knoxville yet, and we will not name one until we have.

Does insurance change the price?

The roof costs what the work costs. Insurance changes who pays: the insurer pays covered work, less your deductible and any depreciation held back. See the claim guide.
