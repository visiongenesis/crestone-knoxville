Real price ranges, how roofers price, and what moves your number.

The number most people want

$10,000 to $18,000

That's the typical range for an asphalt-shingle replacement on a Knoxville-area home. A 2,000 square foot house usually has 25 to 30 squares of roof (a square is 100 square feet). Your free inspection gives you the real number, in writing.

How roofers price

By the square. The rate covers tear-off, materials and labor. Then add what varies: flashing, vents, decking repair and disposal. When you compare quotes, make sure each one lists the number of squares and what's included.

What moves your price

  • Pitch. Steeper roofs have more surface and need more safety gear.
  • Layers. Two old layers cost more to tear off than one.
  • Decking. Rotted boards get replaced, and we show you photos.
  • Ventilation. Doing it right adds a little and adds years.
  • Material. Metal costs more up front and often lasts much longer.

Why a phone quote won't cut it

Pitch, layers and decking don't show up on a satellite photo. In about an hour on the roof, we measure, photograph and write a price you can hold us to.

Financing

Low monthly payment options are available. Ask us at your inspection.

Common questions

Does insurance pay for a new roof?

If storm damage is covered under your policy, it can. See our roof claim guide.

Is metal worth the extra cost?

For many homes, yes, because it lasts much longer. We'll price both at your inspection.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Check your own policy.
