Work with Crestone Roofing in Knoxville

We are building the Knoxville team. Some roles open before others.

ApplyCall (865) 830-8126Text a photo

If you are good at one of the jobs below, talk to us now, even before a role is posted.

Roofing Project Manager

  • Runs each job from the signed contract to the final walk with the homeowner.
  • Sets the schedule, orders the materials and lines up the crew.
  • Books the inspections a job needs and keeps the homeowner updated at every step.
  • Photographs the work as it goes, so every job is on record.
  • We look for someone organized who has run roofing or construction jobs and keeps their word.

Roofing Sales Consultant (estimator)

  • Inspects roofs, takes the photos and writes a price with each part of the job on its own line.
  • When a storm did the damage, documents it for the homeowner's insurance claim: dated photos, an itemized repair estimate and a visit with the adjuster on the roof.
  • Works directly with the homeowner's insurance company and adjuster so the scope covers what the roof actually needs.
  • Follows up with every homeowner until they have the answer they need.
  • We look for someone who knows roofs, tells people the truth about them and writes it down.

Crew Foreman (lead installer)

  • Runs the install crew on site, from tear-off to clean-up.
  • Keeps the crew safe: fall protection, sound ladders and a clear work area.
  • Checks the work before the crew leaves: flashing, nailing and vents, then a sweep of the yard for nails.
  • We look for someone who has led a roofing crew and takes pride in a straight line of shingles.

Roofing Installers

  • Experienced shingle and metal roof installers.
  • Tear-off, decking, underlayment, flashing and finish work, done right on every roof.
  • Subcontract crews that carry their own insurance are welcome to talk to us too.
  • We look for people who show up, work safely and leave a clean site.

Office and Scheduling Coordinator

  • Answers the phones and texts and books same-day inspections.
  • Keeps the schedule for inspections and jobs, and handles permits and paperwork.
  • Asks customers for a review when their job is done.
  • We look for someone friendly on the phone, careful with details and calm on a busy day.
