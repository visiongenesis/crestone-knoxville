About Crestone

Products we install

We can get the major brands in roofing, siding, windows and gutters through the big supply houses in Knoxville. These are the brands we work with. Have one in mind that isn't here? Ask.

Shingles

Asphalt shingles are the standard roof on Knoxville-area homes, and architectural shingles are what most of them get today.

  • Timberline HDZ, Timberline UHDZ, Timberline NS and Timberline AS II shingles, plus Timberline ArmorShield II impact-resistant shingles.

  • Duration, Oakridge and Berkshire shingles, including the Duration FLEX and Duration STORM impact-resistant lines.

  • CertainTeed

    Landmark and Landmark PRO shingles, plus the NorthGate ClimateFlex, Grand Manor and Presidential lines.

  • TAMKO

    Heritage, Titan XT and Elite Glass-Seal shingles, plus StormFighter FLEX.

  • An Independent Contractor, not an Employee or Agent of Atlas Roofing Corporation

    Pinnacle Pristine, Pinnacle Impact and StormMaster Shake shingles, plus ProLam and Briarwood Pro.

  • Cambridge, Dynasty and Nordic shingles, plus the Crowne Slate and Armourshake lines.

  • Vista, Legacy, Highlander and Windsor shingles.

Metal

Metal comes two ways: standing seam, with raised seams and hidden fasteners, and exposed-fastener panels screwed through the face. You see both on farmhouses, cabins and barns.

  • McElroy Metal

    Maxima and Medallion standing seam metal roof panels.

  • Union Corrugating

    MasterRib panels and Snap-Lock standing seam roof panels.

  • Metal Sales

    Standing seam roof systems and roof panels.

Low-slope

Flat and nearly flat roofs take a membrane instead of shingles: TPO, EPDM or a roof coating. You meet them on businesses, churches and the flat part of a home addition.

  • Sure-Weld TPO and Sure-Seal EPDM membranes, plus PVC and FleeceBACK membranes.

  • TPO, PVC and EPDM membranes, including RubberGard EPDM.

  • GAF EverGuard

    EverGuard single-ply membranes, including TPO and PVC.

  • Johns Manville

    JM TPO and JM EPDM single-ply roofing systems.

Skylights

A skylight is a window in the roof, and the flashing around it is where it usually leaks. When the unit itself is worn out, it gets replaced.

  • Fixed skylights, Fresh Air skylights and Sun Tunnel skylights.

Siding

Vinyl, fiber cement and engineered wood cover most houses around Knoxville. Each one handles heat, humidity and a hard freeze differently.

  • Plank lap siding, Panel siding, Hardie Artisan siding, and Shake and Shingle siding.

  • LP SmartSide ExpertFinish engineered wood trim and siding.

  • Mastic

    Mastic vinyl siding, including SolarDefense reflective technology.

Windows and doors

Replacement windows and entry doors, fitted to the frames you already have. The energy ratings are on the label, and we'll explain them in plain words.

  • Andersen

    400 Series, 200 Series, 100 Series, A-Series and E-Series windows.

  • Endure vinyl windows and entry doors.

Gutters

Aluminum gutters in 5-inch and 6-inch sizes, plus downspouts and gutter guards. Wide, steep roofs shed more water and may need the 6-inch size.

  • K-Style gutters and gutter coil, with RainPro, MicroGuard, SureGuard and LeafGuard lines.

  • Berger

    Aluminum, steel, copper and vinyl gutter systems.
