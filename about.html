A Colorado roofing company, starting fresh in Knoxville.

Who we are, what's true today, and what isn't finished yet.

Where Crestone comes from

Crestone Roofing was founded in Colorado Springs in 2018. The company does residential roofing, commercial roofing, storm damage repair, and gutters. It grew a second location serving the Denver Metro area.

The Knoxville operation is new. We're building it with the Crestone team in Colorado. We'd rather tell you that up front than pretend we've been here for years.

Who's running Knoxville

Ryan Dix lives in Knoxville. He spent 20 years in finance and operations at ExxonMobil and Iron Mountain. He is not a lifelong roofer, and he won't tell you he is.

What he brings is the habit of putting things in writing, keeping to a schedule, and showing the numbers.

Licensing

Tennessee contractor licensing is in progress. We'll post the license number on this page when the state issues it. Until then, the work we can take on is limited, and we'll tell you exactly what that means for your job.

How we work

  • We photograph what we find and show you every picture.
  • Every estimate is in writing.
  • If a repair will do, we say so.
  • The insurance claim is yours. We help with photos and the adjuster visit. The claim stays in your hands.
