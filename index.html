What a roof costs around Knoxville

$15,000 to $30,000

A planning range for a typical asphalt shingle replacement on a Knoxville-area home, before we've seen your roof. Steep pitches, a second layer or soft decking push it up. Your free inspection gets you the real number, in writing.

Most homeowners pay with a bank or credit-union loan, a home equity line, or an insurance claim after a storm; we don't have a lender program yet, so say so when you book.
