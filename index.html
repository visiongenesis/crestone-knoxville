Questions

What homeowners ask us.

How much does a new roof cost in Knoxville?

Most asphalt-shingle homes run $10,000 to $18,000. Your free inspection gives you the exact number in writing.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. We get on the roof, take photos, and give you a straight answer. No charge, no obligation.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage, write the scope, and meet your adjuster on the roof with you. You file the claim; we bring the proof.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are done in a single day. We tell you the plan before we start.

Yes. Low monthly payment options are available. Ask us at your inspection.
