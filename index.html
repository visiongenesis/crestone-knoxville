Insurance claims

How a roof claim works in Tennessee

A lot of storm roofs get paid for by insurance. Here is the order things happen, and where we fit in.

  1. We inspect, for free.

    We check the shingles, flashing, vents, and gutters. You get photos and an honest read on whether it looks like storm damage.

  2. You file the claim.

    The claim is between you and your insurance company. Tennessee law doesn't let a roofer act as your adjuster, and we won't try.

  3. The adjuster comes out.

    If you want us there, we'll meet the adjuster on the roof and walk through our photos together.

  4. You decide, in writing.

    If the claim is approved, you get a written estimate that matches the approved scope. If it's denied, Tennessee law gives you the right to cancel.
